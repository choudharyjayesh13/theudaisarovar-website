The perfect 2 days in Udaipur

Two days is enough to fall for Udaipur if you plan it right. This itinerary mixes the classic sights with slow time by the water.

Day 1: Arrive, unwind, the lake

  • Arrival: we pick you up from Udaipur airport (Dabok) or Udaipur City railway station
  • Afternoon: check in, have a Mewari thali lunch, then laze by the lakefront infinity pool
  • Golden hour: a horse ride and photos by the lake
  • Night: bonfire, music, dal baati dinner (or laal maas), and a movie under the sky

Day 2: City of Lakes & Nathdwara

  • Early morning: sunrise chai on the shore of Udai Sagar
  • Morning: Nathdwara darshan with Eklingji and the Statue of Belief
  • Afternoon: City Palace, Jagdish Temple and a boat on Lake Pichola
  • Sunset: Fateh Sagar pal or Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace)
  • Evening: back to the lake for dinner, or a drop to your flight or train

Only have one day?

Stay in: sunrise walk, horse ride, pool, a long Mewari lunch, golden-hour photos, then bonfire and cinema. It's the most relaxing day in Udaipur. Or do a half-day city tour and be back for sunset.

Got a third day?

Add Kumbhalgarh Fort and the Ranakpur Jain temple, or Chittorgarh Fort. We arrange the car and driver.
