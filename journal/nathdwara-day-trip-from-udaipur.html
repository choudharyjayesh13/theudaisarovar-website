A Nathdwara day trip from Udaipur

About 50 km north of Udaipur, the temple town of Nathdwara is one of the most important pilgrimage places for followers of Lord Krishna. It makes an easy and rewarding half-day or full-day trip from the lake.

Shrinathji Temple

Shrinathji, a child form of Krishna, was brought here from Govardhan near Mathura in the 17th century under the protection of the Mewar rulers. The temple follows the Pushtimarg tradition. Darshan happens in short windows called jhankis through the day, starting with Mangla in the early morning and ending with Shayan in the evening. The doors close in between.

Darshan timings change with the season and festivals. Our drivers check the day's schedule and time your drive so you don't wait for hours outside.

Statue of Belief (Vishwas Swaroopam)

Just outside town stands one of the world's tallest statues of Lord Shiva, a 369-foot seated figure on a hill, opened in 2022. You can see it from far away, and you can go inside to the viewing galleries.

Eklingji Temple

On the way from Udaipur, at Kailashpuri, the Eklingji temple complex is dedicated to the ruling deity of Mewar. The Maharanas ruled as his "diwan". The carved temples are beautiful, and it's worth a stop.

Haldighati

Add a short detour to Haldighati, the pass where Maharana Pratap fought the Mughal army in 1576. The museum and the yellow-earth hills tell the story.

What to eat

Nathdwara is famous for its rich rabri, thandai and paan, plus the temple's own prasad. Leave room!

Make it a full day: Nathdwara + Fateh Sagar

  1. Leave early for Eklingji and Shrinathji darshan
  2. See the Statue of Belief, then have lunch
  3. Drive back to Udaipur for an evening on the Fateh Sagar pal
  4. Watch the sunset at Sajjangarh, then return to the lake for dinner
