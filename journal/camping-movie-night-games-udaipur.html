Camping, cinema under the stars & games by the lake

Some of the best nights in Udaipur aren't spent in the city at all. At The Udaisarovar, evenings belong to the lake: tents by the water, a crackling fire, a big screen under the stars and a table full of home-cooked food.

Lakeside camping

Our tents sit on the shore under twin palms, with fairy lights, a bonfire and a sunrise over Udai Sagar that you'll talk about for years. It's perfect for friends, college groups, family reunions and anyone who wants Udaipur under open sky. Breakfast is included.

Movie under the sky

Blankets on the lawn, a big projector screen, popcorn and the lake breeze. Bring your favourite film or let us pick one. Kids love it, and couples love it more.

Bonfire & music nights

As the temperature drops from October to February, the bonfire becomes the centre of everything: songs, stories, snacks off the grill and a sky full of stars.

Horse riding

Gentle rides on the grounds by the lake, plus horse photo shoots at golden hour, popular with families and pre-wedding couples.

Indoor & outdoor games

Badminton and cricket on the lawns, and carrom and board games indoors, for easy, screen-free fun for every age.

Groups & celebrations

Planning a birthday, a team outing or a college trip? We add DJ and lighting by JD Production & Pronite, set menus and private use of the lawns.
