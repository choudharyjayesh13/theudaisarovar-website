Experiences

Days by the pool, nights under the stars

Infinity pool on the lake

The edge of the pool meets Udai Sagar, the signature view of every stay. Two swim sessions daily, 8–11 AM and 4:30–7:30 PM.

Lakeside camping

Tents on the lake shore with a bonfire and live music system. Mattress, bed and blanket, breakfast and campsite assistance are included. Best from October to March; group camps on request.

₹3,000 per tent for two

Bonfire & music nights

Gather round the fire on the lawn with music, with the lights of the lake shimmering across the water.

Cinema under the stars

An open-air projector screening by the lake, every night from 9 PM.

Horse riding

Ride our white horse across the lakeside lawns with our riding partner. Add it to any retreat package for ₹999 per person, or ask at reception.
