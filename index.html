Every booking includes breakfast. Add lunch and dinner to any stay, or order a full Mewari thali. Tell us when you book if you'd like laal maas or other non-veg dishes, and the kitchen will prepare them fresh.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far is The Udaisarovar from Udaipur city?

We're on Udai Sagar Lake at Lakadwas, about 12 km from Fateh Sagar Lake and the main sights, roughly a 30-minute drive. We can arrange airport, railway station and sightseeing transfers.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Every cottage and suite booking includes breakfast. Lunch, dinner, half-board and full-board plans can be added.

Do you serve non-veg and laal maas?

Yes. Our kitchen cooks traditional Mewari vegetarian food daily, and laal maas and other non-veg dishes are prepared fresh on advance order.

Can you arrange a Udaipur city tour or Nathdwara darshan?

Yes. We arrange an AC car with driver for a full or half-day Udaipur city tour, Nathdwara darshan (with Eklingji and the Statue of Belief), or a combined Nathdwara + Fateh Sagar day.

Do you offer airport or railway station pickup?

Yes. Pickup and drop from Udaipur airport (Dabok), Udaipur City railway station, the bus stand or anywhere in the city. Share your timings on WhatsApp.

What activities are there at the resort?

Horse riding and horse photography, the lakeside infinity pool, camping, movie under the sky, bonfire and music nights, indoor and outdoor games, DJ nights on request, and sunrise birdwatching.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged when rooms are free.

How do I pay?

Book on this website, or pay online by card or UPI (see Pay online), or ask us for a payment link on WhatsApp. Diwali and New Year stays need a minimum of 2 nights.
