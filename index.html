Why The Udaisarovar

A lakeside hotel in Udaipur that feels like your own

Looking for the best lakeside hotel in Udaipur without the five-star price tag? The Udaisarovar sits right on the water of Udaisagar Lake at Lakadwas, a short drive from Udaipur's City Palace and Fateh Sagar — close enough for sightseeing, far enough for real peace and open sky.

We're one of the most reasonable lakeside stays in Udaipur: private lake-view cottages start at just ₹4,500 with breakfast, and you can take the entire villa for a group from ₹18,000. Every stay comes with an infinity pool on the lakefront, bonfire evenings, lakeside tented camping and home-cooked Rajasthani food — the kind of Udaipur experience the big hotels can't match.

Whether it's a couple's weekend, a family holiday, a corporate offsite or a lakeside wedding, booking direct on this site is always the lowest price — no travel-site commission, best-rate guaranteed.
